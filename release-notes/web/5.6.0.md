- Recommendations update as you subscribe and listen. Edit sits with the media chips. Pick a
  weight from 1 to 5, and hiding a show tells you it moved off recommendations. A podcast listen
  counts after about 5 minutes. Music counts after about a minute
- While you are signed in on more than one device, the player shows what is playing there. You can
  pause, skip, seek, and move playback between them. Closing the browser that was playing stops
  the others from following it after a short moment. A tab that is not actually playing elsewhere
  plays here again. Scrubbing seeks once when you release the bar. Playback Speed lists every rate.
  The page stays lined up under the player and fits the window, so lists and the footer do not
  scroll sideways. The queue puts the loaded item under Now Playing,
  even when it was never queued. The mini player keeps its buttons when another device is
  playing, and long titles stay within the page. The account menu opens below your name
- While a livestream plays, Live replaces the length. Rewind appears when the stream allows it,
  and Play after a pause continues there or jumps back to live. Livestream rows hide the progress
  bar. An ended livestream stays ended, and a live show can play with no length
- Empty lists name what is missing, such as No Podcasts Found. List rows show the date without
  the time, and mixed lists put the show name above the title. On a playing row, progress fills
  beside the date. Profiles use chips. Unseen dots sit on the artwork. Playing or opening a
  row clears that row's dot for the rest of the visit. A wrong password is shown in bold.
  Unread count sits on the notifications bell corner. An email with capital letters still signs
  in. Signed out, home says your subscriptions will appear here, with Login on that line, and
  Search and Browse under it
- Browse opens on the past week's top results. When that top list is empty, Browse by Most
  Recent opens the recent list. Pressing a category chip clears that category.
  About, Podroll, People, and Remote Items are chips on a show page, and Podroll still lists
  shows that are not in the directory yet. A new browser asks once to allow push
- Trial and Premium can each save up to 100 feeds you add by RSS. At that limit, the page says
  so and offers Email Support. The same address with or without a slash at the end of the path
  is one follow. The membership comparison marks Add Custom RSS Feeds under Free and Premium.
  Free is the mobile app. Adding a feed on the website still needs a membership. The Trial
  limitations list no longer includes a separate Add by RSS cap
