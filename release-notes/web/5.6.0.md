- Boost a podcast, an episode, or a tip on the donate page without an account. Your name and
  message stay locked until you sign in. A signed-in boost includes that message when the show
  has a lightning node, including a show with no MetaBoost tag. Boost is on episode, track, and
  Add by RSS item pages, and in the full player
- Next and Previous move as soon as you tap them. Each episode you skip stays in your history,
  even if it never started playing. Previous at the start of an episode steps back to the one
  you just left. After that, Previous starts the episode over
- Signed out, the site opens on Browse: Podcasts, sorted by Recent, in a grid. The logo goes
  there too. Signed in, home is still your subscriptions, and the logo goes there. A choice you
  already saved, or a link that names the page, still wins. Lists such as Browse show the pages
  they have loaded, and a full last page does not open onto an empty one
- An RSS address Podverse already has opens that podcast and subscribes you. Refreshing Add by
  RSS does the same for feeds you already saved, and your queue and playlists move with them.
  That feed does not count toward your Add by RSS limit. Adding one while a refresh is already
  parsing it opens the show. A password the feed rejects stays explained. History lists Add by
  RSS episodes and tracks with your other listens. From there you can play, queue next, queue
  last, add to a playlist, or mark played
- While you are signed in on more than one device, you can pause, skip, seek, and move playback
  between them. Scrubbing seeks the file that is loaded. Closing the browser that was playing
  stops the others from following it after a short moment
- Finished episodes, clips, and soundbites show a Played check beside the progress bar. Show
  notes and About keep their paragraphs, links, and lists. While a livestream plays, Live
  replaces the length, and rewind appears when the stream allows it. An ended livestream stays
  ended
