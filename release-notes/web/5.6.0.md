- While a livestream plays, Live replaces the length. Rewind appears when the stream allows it,
  and Play after a pause continues there or jumps back to live. An ended livestream stays out of
  the player. A signed-in tab that is not actually playing elsewhere plays here again
- Podroll and Remote Items list shows that are not in the directory yet. Add by RSS feeds get the
  same lists. A click opens the show, or a page where you can add a feed the directory does not
  know
- Browse opens on the past week's top results and says when a list is empty. Public playlists can
  be sorted by most recently updated
- While you are signed in on more than one device, the player shows what is playing there. You can
  pause, skip, seek, and move playback between them
- Notifications are one line with artwork. Unseen dots stay while the inbox is open. A new browser
  asks once to allow push. Turning on a show still turns its notifications on if you decline push,
  and settings warn when this browser cannot receive push
- A media chip selects right away. Search and filters clear with an X. Player settings are a More
  menu. Dates include the time, and transcripts can follow playback. Add by RSS stays in your
  library, and a missing show or episode shows Not Found
