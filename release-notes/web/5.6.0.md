- Find people by name, see who is popular, and follow someone without notifications. A show
  lists its cast and guests, including a show you added by RSS. Recommendations can include
  people. A portrait that cannot load shows a placeholder
- Add tags when you create or edit a playlist. They show on the playlist and in lists. Public
  playlists can be filtered by one tag. Signed out, Playlists opens on Recent. A choice you
  saved, or a link that names the page, still wins
- Turn on an authenticator app for sign-in. After it is on, an email code or a backup code can
  finish sign-in
- Boost a podcast, an episode, or a tip on the donate page without an account. Your name and
  message stay locked until you sign in. A signed-in boost includes that message when the show
  has a lightning address, including a show with no MetaBoost tag
- Next and Previous move as soon as you tap them. Each episode you skip stays in your history.
  Previous at the start of an episode steps back. While you are signed in on more than one
  device, you can pause, skip, and seek from another one. Opening Search puts the cursor in the
  field. Signed out, the site opens on Browse: Podcasts, sorted by Recent, in a grid. On a wide
  screen, the page column stops stretching
- An RSS address Podverse already has opens that podcast and subscribes you. Your queue and
  playlists move with it, and that feed does not count toward your Add by RSS limit. History
  lists those episodes with your other listens. Finished episodes show a Played check. Show
  notes and About keep their paragraphs, links, and lists. Opening a show keeps the episode
  list up while it loads. While a livestream plays, Live replaces the length
