- An RSS address Podverse already has opens that podcast and subscribes you. Refreshing Add by
  RSS does the same for feeds you already saved, and your queue and playlists move with them.
  That feed does not count toward your Add by RSS limit. Adding one while a refresh is already
  parsing it opens the show instead of an error. A password the feed rejects stays explained
- While you are signed in on more than one device, you can pause, skip, seek, and move playback
  between them. Scrubbing seeks once when you release, and it seeks the file that is loaded.
  Closing the browser that was playing stops the others from following it after a short moment
- Finished episodes, clips, and soundbites show a Played check beside the progress bar. Mark as
  Played updates the check in the list
- Show notes and About keep their paragraphs, links, and lists
- While a livestream plays, Live replaces the length. Rewind appears when the stream allows it,
  and Play after a pause continues there or jumps back to live. An ended livestream stays ended
- Choosing Remote Items, Podroll, or another section on a podcast or album puts that section in
  the address, so a shared link opens it. The section the page opens on leaves the address
  clean. Recommendations update as you subscribe and listen. The usual follow limit is 500
  podcasts, playlists, and accounts, counted together
