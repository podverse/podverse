- Recommendations update as you listen. Edit Recommendations sits with the media chips. Hide a
  suggestion, or mark a show Not Counted in Recommendations, from the list or Settings. A podcast
  listen counts after about 5 minutes. Music counts after about a minute
- While you are signed in on more than one device, the player shows what is playing there. You can
  pause, skip, seek, and move playback between them. Closing the browser that was playing stops
  the others from following it after a short moment. A tab that is not actually playing elsewhere
  plays here again
- While a livestream plays, Live replaces the length. Rewind appears when the stream allows it,
  and Play after a pause continues there or jumps back to live. An ended livestream stays out of
  the player. A live show can play even when it has no length
- Queues and History say when they are empty. On episode and clip rows, More sits between Play and
  the date. Unseen episode counts sit on the artwork in the same blue as the unseen dots, and the
  notification bell uses that blue too
- Browse opens on the past week's top results. Podroll and Remote Items list shows that are not in
  the directory yet. Notifications are one line with artwork. A new browser asks once to allow
  push, and settings warn when this browser cannot receive push
- A media chip selects right away, and Sort and the layout control stay up while the list loads.
  Escape closes an open Sort or filter menu. Search and filters clear with an X. Dates include the
  time, and transcripts can follow playback
