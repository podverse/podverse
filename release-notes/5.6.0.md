- Signed-in members can play on one device and control it from the web or the phone. Other
  devices show that playback and can pause, seek, skip, or move it. Routes live under
  `/account/playback`. When a browser's event stream ends, that session leaves playback presence
  after `PLAYBACK_PRESENCE_LEAVE_GRACE_MS` (base `api.env` uses 5000; web end-to-end uses 500)
  and cannot check back in until a newer stream of the same session opens
- A live item a feed drops is stored as ended. Players show Live, time behind live, and rewind
  only when the stream keeps a window. Pause resumes inside that window or jumps to live. An
  ended livestream is not restored. A live item with no duration can start. On the web, Play
  works again in a signed-in tab that is not actually playing elsewhere
- For You rebuilds a listener's channel recommendations when a show is added to the Taste
  Profile, its 1–5 weight changes, Settings applies an override, or a show is removed. The
  similarity graph still runs nightly. Hide keeps a show off Browse and Recommendations without
  changing Taste Profile influence or subscriptions. Not Counted in Recommendations is the list
  of shows left out of scoring; those shows stay visible. A non-music listen counts at 5 minutes,
  or at half the length when that is shorter. Music stays at 1 minute, or half the length of a
  track under 2 minutes. The player-loaded length is used when it is between half and double the
  RSS duration. App migration `0003_stats_channel_hidden.sql`
- Podroll, remote items, and Not Yet Added albums return stored remote stubs instead of a live
  Podcast Index lookup. New reads: `GET /remote-item/channel/:idOrIdText`,
  `POST /account/add-by-rss/remote-item/lookup`, and `.../resolve`. Management can re-check,
  re-point, promote, or delete a stub. App migration `0002_remote_stubs.sql`. Leave
  `PARSER_ADD_REMOTE_ITEMS_TO_MQ` empty for stubs (`true` queues a full parse and writes none).
  Browse Top returns only rows with listens in that range. Livestream Top, Subscribed Top, and a
  show's episode Top still include zero counts. Public playlists add `GET /playlist/public/recent`.
  Notifications stay one line with artwork and a relative time. Unseen inbox dots last for the
  visit. A new browser or phone asks once to allow push. A show's notifications still turn on if
  push is declined. Settings warn when this device cannot receive push. Test pushes run only
  where `NOTIFICATIONS_TEST_PUSH_ENABLED=true`
- On mobile, pressing play on the lock screen, headset, or CarPlay after a pause reloads a failed
  item once at its playhead. A listener who had paused is not resumed when a call ends, and
  another app that is playing keeps the headphones. Transcripts open without a list error,
  including Add by RSS in the full player. Video fullscreen waits until the host has laid out.
  VoiceOver titles stay inside their controls. A refresh already in flight at sign-out is ignored
  once a newer session has started. On web and mobile, empty Queues and History say so. On the
  web, unseen counts sit on artwork in the accent color, episode-row More sits between play and
  the date, Sort stays up while a media chip's list loads, and Escape closes a menu opened from
  its chip, including in management. The address stays on a signed-in Settings tab until the
  You've Been Signed Out dialog is closed
- Operators, first boot of 5.6.0: drop and recreate the database. Bootstrap applies
  `0001_app_schema.sql` and `0001_management_schema.sql`. Existing databases then run app
  migrations `0002_remote_stubs.sql` and `0003_stats_channel_hidden.sql`. Set
  `API_EXPECTED_MIGRATION_FILENAME` to `0003_stats_channel_hidden.sql` and
  `MANAGEMENT_API_EXPECTED_MIGRATION_FILENAME` to `0001_management_schema.sql`. Rename the
  popularity agreement env vars to `TASTE_PROFILE_AGREEMENT_VERSION` and
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_TASTE_PROFILE_AGREEMENT_VERSION` (default `2026-10-21`). Remove
  `AUTH_JWT_EXPIRATION`, `AUTH_MOBILE_ACCESS_TOKEN_EXPIRATION`,
  `AUTH_MOBILE_REFRESH_TOKEN_EXPIRATION`, and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SIDEBAR_GROUP_ORDER`. Session length
  is `AUTH_SESSION_IDLE_EXPIRATION` (31536000). Access tokens are `AUTH_ACCESS_TOKEN_EXPIRATION`
  (900). Listen records are kept `STATS_TRACK_EVENT_RETENTION_DAYS` (90). After the wipe,
  recreate the management superuser and billing rows. `BILLING_SANDBOX_GRANT_ALL=true` grants
  Premium for every sandbox purchase on a test deployment only. Tracing and extension metrics
  use OpenTelemetry SDK 3. `OTEL_TRACES_SAMPLER` and `OTEL_TRACES_SAMPLER_ARG` are applied in
  process when set
