## Taste Profile

Taste Profile is an optional feature. It is available with a Premium membership. It is built from your listens, and those listens also count toward public popularity rankings without your name. It stays off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off in account settings. Turning it off does not limit playback, your account, or a membership.

When it is on, {legal_name} records a listen once you have heard 60 seconds, or 25% of the episode or track, whichever comes first. Each counted listen is its own record. The record includes the episode or track, the clip when you played a clip, the show it belongs to, how many seconds you heard, whether you finished it, and the time. Hearing it again can count again. A new listen that arrives too soon after your last recorded one is not recorded. There is a daily limit on how many listens are recorded. A lower daily limit sets how many of them count toward the public lists; listens past it still update your Taste Profile. Listening that looks automated can be left out of the public lists; it still updates your Taste Profile. On the website, opening someone else's profile is recorded, and opening a playlist is recorded. Those opens count toward public lists only. They do not update your Taste Profile. There is a daily limit on how many of those opens are recorded. Opening your own profile is not recorded. The mobile apps do not record those opens. Listening while signed out is not recorded this way.

Each record stores a random id for your account. The id stays with your account. It is not your name, your email, or your public username. {legal_name} can connect that id back to your account, so your choice and a deletion request apply to you. The record is not anonymous.

{legal_name} also keeps a Taste Profile: one row per show you listen to (a podcast, an album, or an artist). Episodes and tracks are not their own rows. A listen to an episode, a track, or a clip updates the show it belongs to. A row keeps how many times you have listened, how many you finished, when you first and last listened, and a picture of what you have been listening to lately and over a longer time. You can see this list in account settings. You can remove a show from it. The show then moves to Not Counted in Recommendations, and its taste data is deleted. Later listens to a show on that list still count on the public lists, and they do not add the show back. Taking a show off Not Counted in Recommendations asks you to add it back to your Taste Profile, starting over, or only to take it off that list. If you only take it off the list, a later listen can add it again. Opening a profile or a playlist does not update your Taste Profile.

The records and the Taste Profile are used to count listeners for discovery lists, so creators can see that their work is reaching people, and so recommendations can reflect shows you listen to. They are not sold, your Taste Profile is not shared with anyone else, and they are not used to advertise to you. Public lists show counts. They do not show who listened, and those counts are not tied to your account. Each list counts you at most once per episode, show, clip, profile, or playlist. The day, week, and month lists are counted from stored records, so when your records are deleted, your listening leaves those lists. The all-time count adds you again only after a set period.

Listen records are kept for {retention_days} days. {legal_name} then deletes a whole month of records at once, so a record can remain until its month is removed. Your Taste Profile is not on that schedule. It stays until you turn Taste Profile off or you delete your account. All-time counts already published stay as numbers, without a list of who listened.

Turning Taste Profile off asks you to confirm. On confirm, {legal_name} deletes your Taste Profile, your Not Counted in Recommendations list, and your stored listen records right away. Public all-time counts already published stay. If you turn it on later, recommendations start from scratch. If you turn it on again, the daily limits do not start over, and for a set period after you turned it off, your new listens update your Taste Profile but do not count toward the public lists, and opening a profile or a playlist is not recorded. If your membership ends, or if this explanation changes and you have not confirmed it yet, new records stop and your Taste Profile stays paused until tracking is on again. Your Not Counted in Recommendations list stays. Listen records already stored still age out on that schedule. The Taste Profile is not deleted in those cases. If you turn it off, {legal_name} will not ask again when the explanation changes. If you turn it on, and what is recorded or how long it is kept later changes, you will be asked again. Until you say yes, tracking stays off.

Deleting your account deletes the id, your listen records, your Taste Profile, and your Not Counted in Recommendations list. The public all-time counts stay.
